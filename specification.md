# Specification

Offer Management is **RCP-57**, proposed on RESO Transport.

<div class="provenance">
  <p class="pr-link"><a href="https://github.com/RESOStandards/transport/issues/252">Read and discuss the proposal on RESO Transport: issue 252</a></p>
  <p>The proposal text lives at <code>proposals/offer-management.md</code> in <code>RESOStandards/transport</code>. Review and discussion happen there, and that copy is authoritative.</p>
</div>

## Status

The proposal is open. A reference implementation exists and is described on the [Tools](/tools/) page, and a [playground](/playground/) runs the activity layer in your browser.

Two implementations advance a standard, and more is better. If you are building against this, say so at [transport@reso.org](mailto:transport@reso.org).
